It's always remarkable how quickly time flies. Last year around this time, I remember walking through the Stuttgart west and admiring the stone facades of buildings. It was similar time, early August, I was done settling in and had started working on understanding my PhD topic. Now, I am a little more comfortable in the city, can navigate myself without need of google maps most of the time. 

I need to reflect more. I wanted to articulate the feeling, but I am lost for words. I do not think language is the rate limiting here, but something else. Why sometimes it is hard to simply put my head down and work, work and work. The non-essential life things - water, food, fatigue and brain fog, why cannot I control them? 

Hopefully, I cringe and laugh at past me, when I look at this entry after couple of years. If not something pityful must have happened.

On the bright side, a lot of new things have happened in my non-academic life. I cherrish them a lot.
